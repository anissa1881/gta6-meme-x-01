## The meme

`meme.png` is the meme: a Vice City sunset boulevard, a teal convertible, and make.fun's ghost behind the wheel. The caption reads "GTA 6 LAUNCH DAY" / "sorry boss, I'm stuck in Vice City". The ghost is make.fun's real mascot from the press kit, composited into the scene.

## The post

The meme is posted on X from a public account. The post's text carries a link to make.fun, a tag of @makefunai, and the account's play code, and it tells readers that people and their AI agents can earn real money in make.fun bounties. `POST.json` at the repo root names the post's address.

## How this entry works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `POST.json` to the output directory, where the house reads it and fetches the post from X. That is all the program does — the post itself is the entry.
